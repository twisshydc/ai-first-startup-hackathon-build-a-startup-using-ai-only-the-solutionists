THE MĀNUKA GUIDE

Good honey.
Better understanding.

Two common terms you may see when comparing Mānuka products.

MGO

What is MGO?

Methylglyoxal (MGO) is a naturally occurring compound measured in Mānuka honey. Products may display an MGO number indicating its measured concentration.

UMF

What is UMF?

UMF is a grading and certification system used for qualifying Mānuka honey. Check a product's documentation rather than assuming it has certification.
