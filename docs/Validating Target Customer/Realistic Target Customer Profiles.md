## *Company Profiles:*

###  **Peoplebox.ai**
   
   Peoplebox.ai is a B2B HR-tech SaaS company that provides solutions for performance management, OKRs, employee engagement, and related HR processes. As a B2B SaaS company selling to organizations, it needs to identify businesses that fit its ideal customer profile and reach relevant HR and business decision-makers. This makes it a potential customer for an AI-powered SDR that can automate prospect identification, company research, lead qualification, and prioritization.

### **Cone** 

   Cone is a B2B SaaS company focused on workflow and business automation. As a growing technology company selling its solution to other businesses, its sales team needs to continuously identify relevant prospects and understand whether they are suitable potential customers. An AI-powered SDR could assist by finding high-fit companies, researching their requirements, and prioritizing prospects for the sales team.

### Landeed

   Landeed is a technology company operating in the real-estate and property-data space, providing digital solutions related to property information and transactions. Its specialized product requires identifying businesses and customers with specific property-related needs. An AI-powered SDR could help its sales team discover relevant prospects, analyze their business needs, and determine which accounts are most likely to benefit from its solutions.

### Atomicwork

   Atomicwork is a B2B enterprise software company focused on AI-powered IT service management and employee support. Its product is primarily relevant to organizations with specific IT-service and employee-support requirements, meaning its sales team needs to identify suitable companies and decision-makers. An AI-powered SDR could automate account discovery, prospect research, qualification, and prioritization, allowing the sales team to focus on high-potential opportunities.

### Gojiberry AI

   Gojiberry AI is an AI-powered go-to-market company focused on helping B2B businesses with sales and customer acquisition activities. Since its own target market consists of B2B companies, identifying the right prospects and understanding their business needs are central to its growth. An AI-powered SDR could support this process by automating prospect discovery, enrichment, qualification, and prioritization, making Gojiberry a particularly relevant example for validating the proposed solution.

### 100X
   
   100X is a small AI/B2B technology startup operating with a lean team. For an early-stage company with limited sales resources, manually finding and researching potential customers can consume significant time that could otherwise be spent on product development and closing deals. An AI-powered SDR could help automate repetitive outbound-sales activities such as prospect discovery, company research, qualification, and lead prioritization.
