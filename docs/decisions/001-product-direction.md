# Product Direction
**Date:** 8 August 2026  
**Status:** Validation in progress

## Decision
The team selected **AI Revenue / Sales Intelligence** as the strongest initial direction.

## Problem
Small B2B companies spend significant time manually finding, researching, qualifying, and preparing outreach for potential customers.

## Initial Customer
Small/early-stage B2B SaaS companies with limited or no dedicated sales teams.

## Product Hypothesis
An AI workflow could reduce the manual work involved in prospect discovery, research, qualification, and personalised outreach.

## Initial MVP Direction
```text
Company + ICP
     ↓
Prospect Discovery
     ↓
AI Research & Qualification
     ↓
Prioritised Prospects
     ↓
Personalised Outreach
```

## Why This Direction
* Strong business pain
* Clear potential paying customer
* Potentially measurable ROI
* Strong AI application
* Realistic MVP

## What Must Be Validated
* Customer pain
* Existing competitors
* Data availability
* Technical feasibility
* Willingness to pay
* Potential differentiation

## Important
This is a working hypothesis, not a final product specification.  
The MVP will be locked after the research milestone.
